Anticipated questions

Judge Q&A — straight answers

Is the data real or synthetic?

Discovery is live & grounded with citations. Synthetic seeds are a labelled demo-safety net. Private banking fields are honestly absent — never fabricated.

Could the AI hallucinate a recommendation?

The score is deterministic; the LLM only phrases prose and is screened by a guardrail. A human approves before anything is used.

Is it secure & compliant for a bank?

RBAC, JWT, Workload Identity Federation (no stored keys), full audit trail, governed weight changes, secret/dependency scanning in CI.

Will it scale across Europe?

Stateless service on Cloud Run + Firestore; one engine across sectors & corridors; connectors are additive and feature-flagged.

How is bias mitigated?

Transparent weights, evidence per driver, missing-data penalties, corroboration checks — decisions are inspectable and challengeable.

What did you actually build vs. plan?

All shown features are built, tested (132) and deployed. Roadmap items are clearly separated as future scope.
